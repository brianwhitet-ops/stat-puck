# Slab app concept 01 - screen map

## Core loop
1. **Today** - current handicap trend, latest-round summary, biggest opportunity, next practice focus.
2. **Rounds** - chronological round history; course, date, score, strokes gained, fairways, GIR, putts. Tap into one round.
3. **Progress** - round-over-round trend lines and rolling averages. Change metric and time range; compare recent form with personal baseline.
4. **Ask Slab** - natural-language questions grounded in the golfer's own rounds. Answers cite the stats behind the advice and turn the answer into a practice plan.
5. **Round detail** - scorecard and category breakdown, then hole-level evidence where useful.
6. **Practice plan** - one or two focused drills, frequency, and a simple completion log. Progress connects back to round outcomes.
7. **Profile / connections** - handicap baseline, bag/tee preferences, hardware sync, data permissions, export and delete.

## Navigation
Persistent tabs: Today / Rounds / Progress / Ask

## Product rules
- Improvement first, data second. Every screen answers “what changed?” or “what should I work on?”
- Keep mid-round use on the hardware. The app is for post-round review, progression, and questions.
- Explain every recommendation with visible evidence from the golfer's stats.
- Do not pretend precision the hardware did not collect. Mark estimates and comparisons clearly.
- Calm Field Lab palette: warm paper, graphite, one muted green signal color, oversized numbers, thin rules.

## Locked navigation names - Sep 15
Bench / Rounds / Stats / Curve / Lab

- Bench: home readout and next focus
- Rounds: chronological round record
- Stats: all captured inputs and derived round stats
- Curve: progression and trends
