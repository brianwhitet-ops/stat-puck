01 / Play

The phone is a terrible scoring instrument.

Unlock tax

Glove, glare, lock screen.

Mid-hole you are managing a rectangle that wants a password, a brightness slider, and both thumbs. The next shot waits.

Split attention

The card should not pull you out.

Scoring is a glance, not a session. If the instrument argues with you, you stop keeping an honest card — or you stop playing the hole in front of you.

Empty review

Improvement needs a card you kept.

Greens, putting, versus par — those numbers only help if they come from a round you actually finished. Focus first. Review later.

Slab is a different habit. Score on the cart. Phone in the bag. Attention on the hole.

02 / Product
